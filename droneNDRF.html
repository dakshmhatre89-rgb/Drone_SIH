The Golden 72 Hours

Every excavation hour spent guessing is an hour a survivor doesn't have. Here's the bottleneck we built Lifeline to remove.

// The Problem

The Search Bottleneck

K9 units and manual acoustic listening remain the industry standard — and they're painfully slow. Clearing just a few square meters of collapsed structure can take hours of careful, methodical sweeps, while heavy machinery noise, dust, and unstable debris drown out the faint signs of life beneath.

Survival rates fall below 10–15% once the 72-hour mark passes. Every hour spent searching blind is excavation time a trapped survivor cannot spare.

System Architecture

Tech Pillars & Features

Ten integrated systems, one edge-deployed platform. Built to run in the rubble, not the cloud.

01

Multimodal Sensor Fusion

Combines UWB radar, LWIR thermal imaging, and acoustic geophones to cross-verify human life signs from independent physical channels.

02

AI-Driven Noise Filtering

Deep-learning models strip out deafening heavy-machinery noise on-site to isolate faint human taps or gasps in real time.

03

Micro-Doppler Vital Detection

Tracks chest-cavity micro-movements through solid concrete to confirm heartbeats in unconscious, unresponsive victims.

04

Autonomous Void Exploration

Compact tracked UGVs and micro-drones built to crawl through gaps where neither rescuers nor K9 units can fit.

05

Electronic Nose (Chemical Sensing)

Gas sensors detect unique biomarkers — acetone, CO₂, and ammonia — released in human breath and sweat under duress.

06

Robust Mesh Communications

Sub-GHz ad-hoc mesh networking keeps data flowing through heavy concrete back to the command post, node to node.

07

Real-Time 3D SLAM Mapping

Builds live 3D visual models of subterranean voids as robots move through them — no GPS signal required underground.

08

Edge Computing Architecture

Runs lightweight inference directly on-site via edge AI hardware, eliminating cloud dependency entirely.

09

Rescuer Safety Prioritization

Maps structural stability and toxic gas hazards before first responders ever set foot inside a void.

10

High SWaP-C Optimization

Engineered for low size, weight, power, and cost — fast to deploy for local fire departments worldwide.

The Competitive Advantage

Why Lifeline Wins

Single-sensor tools guess. Lifeline confirms — and never wastes an excavation crew's hours on a false alarm.

01

Tri-Factor Verification

Unlike single-sensor tools, Lifeline won't flag a location unless at least two independent vectors — heat, heartbeat, and sound — match a human signature. No wasted excavation hours chasing false positives.

02

Zero Infrastructure Reliance

A self-contained, secure radio network with on-board hardware processing operates flawlessly even when every 4G and 5G tower nearby has been destroyed.

03

Active Void Penetration

Surface scanning arrays work alongside autonomous micro-bots that crawl directly into structural cracks, bringing sensors closer to the victim for unmatched signal clarity — not passive scanning from above.
